# PlanExe for Law Enforcement & Complex Case Planning

## Summary

PlanExe's structured pipeline output makes it a strong fit for law enforcement agencies, federal prosecutors, and licensed PMCs that need to plan and document complex multi-party cases — RICO investigations, corruption networks, high-value target (HVT) extraction operations, and international treaty-compliant apprehension planning.

## The Problem

Complex cases — a corrupt municipal government protecting an unlicensed operator, a racketeering enterprise with financial, political, and operational layers, a cross-jurisdictional HVT with compromised local law enforcement — require planning across dozens of simultaneous dimensions:

- Legal basis and applicable statutes (federal, state, treaty)
- Target network mapping (associates, financials, cover identities)
- Operational security and compartmentalization
- Evidence chain of custody
- Public communications and counter-narrative
- Custody transfer and jurisdictional handoff

Currently, DA offices and federal task forces manage this across whiteboards, spreadsheets, and disconnected memos. There is no structured, reproducible planning tool built for this problem.

## What PlanExe Does Differently

PlanExe generates a structured, modular operations plan (OPPLAN) from a natural language brief. Each module is a discrete task in the pipeline:

| Module | Output |
|---|---|
| Legal basis | Applicable statutes, treaty coordination, jurisdictional analysis |
| Target network | Associates, financials, cover identities, leverage assets |
| Operations grid | Jurisdiction selection, arrest corridor, diplomatic risk |
| ISR/movement | Surveillance plan, secure comms, asset movement tracking |
| COA development | Course-of-action options with risk/diplomatic hedges |
| OpSec/OpSaf | Secure comms protocol, evidence capture, safe-hand staging |
| Custody transfer | Transport plan, DOJ handoff, treaty-compliant documentation |
| Counter-narrative | Public messaging, propaganda mitigation, press strategy |

The output is a complete, documented OPPLAN with SOP and OpSec/OpSaf annexes — reusable as a template for future cases of the same type.

## Proof of Concept: The BAT Run

The BAT scenario (codename: BATMAN) demonstrates PlanExe's capability on a complex fictional target:

**Target profile:**
- Cover identity: billionaire CEO with public-facing legitimate business
- Base: confirmed underground complex, location known
- Primary hazard: unlicensed nuclear-powered vehicle — radiological threat requiring Priority Zero neutralization before any other action
- Political protection: compromised city officials (mayor, police commissioner) — treat as hostile
- Associate network: juvenile accomplice (child endangerment charges), criminal associate (leverage asset), long-term enabler (highest-value informant target)
- Counterintelligence threat: direct tie to international criminal organization with state-level connections
- Public narrative: active propaganda ecosystem requiring counter-narrative strategy

**Applicable charges identified by PlanExe:**
- 18 U.S.C. § 831 — unlawful possession/transfer of radiological material
- 18 U.S.C. § 842 — unlicensed explosive/dispersal device
- Atomic Energy Act (NRC/DOE) — unlicensed nuclear transport and operation
- 18 U.S.C. § 2332a — WMD use (if reactor deployed offensively)
- 18 U.S.C. § 1361 — willful destruction of government property
- 18 U.S.C. §§ 201, 1512, 1513 — bribery and obstruction (compromised officials)
- 18 U.S.C. § 371 — conspiracy
- 18 U.S.C. §§ 1961–1968 — RICO (full enterprise: bribery, obstruction, nuclear violations, violent crimes)
- Child endangerment statutes — recruitment of minors into violent operations

PlanExe mapped all of this from a single natural language brief, producing a structured OPPLAN with legal, operational, and counter-narrative modules.
